We’re building a live translation device that utilizes a microphone and Head-Up Display (HUD) to turn spoken foreign speech into real-time English captions, all housed in a pair of glasses. A companion app learns user style to suggest relevant replies, which are displayed phonetically for easy pronunciation on the HUD. The HUDini will enable fluid two-way conversation, accelerate language learning, and generally facilitate communication across various languages. Planned deliverables: working product with live speech translation and response suggestion.
