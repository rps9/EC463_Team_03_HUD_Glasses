Hi, we're Team 3, and this is HUDini.

HUDini is a pair of AR glasses that translates conversations in real time. When someone speaks to you in another language, the glasses transcribe their speech, translate it to English, and display the result on a small screen in the corner of your vision. The companion app then suggests a reply, written phonetically, so you can respond without needing to know the language fluently.

For example, if someone says "¿dónde está el baño?", you see the English translation on the lens, along with a phonetic spelling of an appropriate reply such as "el bah-NYOH es-TAH ah-YEE."

The system runs on a pair of Brilliant Frame glasses connected over Bluetooth to an iPhone app we built in SwiftUI. The microphone on the glasses streams 8kHz audio in small BLE packets to the phone, where Apple's on-device speech recognition handles transcription and Apple's Translation framework handles translation. Reply suggestions are generated by GPT using recent conversation context, then passed through a phonemizer we wrote from scratch. The phonemizer supports eleven languages and runs fully offline.

Privacy was a priority. Speech recognition, translation, and phonetic conversion all run locally on the phone. The only component that requires a network connection is the reply suggestion service.

On the hardware side, we developed two parallel builds. The primary demo uses the Brilliant Frame platform. The second build uses a Nordic MDBT42Q module, a PDM microphone, and a custom PCB, running the same BLE protocol and packet format as the Frame so that either device works with the same iOS app.

There are limitations we are continuing to work on. Performance drops in noisy environments, recognition accuracy varies by language at the 8kHz sample rate, and the display is monochrome with limited resolution.

The goal of HUDini is to make cross-language conversation feel natural, both for travelers and for anyone learning a new language. We invite you to try the demo, speak to one of us in another language, and see the system in action. We're available throughout the event to walk through the code, the hardware, or our design decisions. Thank you for stopping by.
